# Neuroscience & Active Inference Reading Group 🧠

Welcome to our reading group!

This group brings together people interested in neuroscience, mathematical biology, and the theoretical foundations of cognition, with a particular focus on **Active Inference** and the **Free Energy Principle (FEP)**.

Our main goal is to explore how perception, learning, and action can be understood within a unified mathematical framework, and to critically examine its applications across biological systems.

## 📚 Reading Sessions

We aim to cover the book *Active Inference* in approximately **10 sessions**, depending on the pace of our discussions.

- Each member is expected to lead at least one presentation on an assigned section of the book.
- Sessions will involve presentations, open discussions, and critical examination of the underlying concepts.
- Active participation is expected from all members.

## 🔬 Beyond the Reading Group

We hope to go beyond simply reading the book. Interesting questions and unresolved problems arising during our discussions may develop into **computational simulations, collaborative research projects, or even publications**.

## 🤝 Collaboration

This GitHub repository serves as a shared workspace for reading notes, presentations, mathematical derivations, code, and research ideas.

Members are welcome to create their own branches, contribute materials, and collaborate through pull requests.

**Let's read, question, and explore together!**
